yhwh begin — Draft implementation instructions from a brief kanban task

Usage: yhwh begin [TODO] [--list]

With no name, choose a COVENANT or kanban/ item interactively. The agent inspects
the repository read-only and writes tmp/NAME.md with the original task, intended
result, implementation, genuine questions, blockers and assumptions. It never
executes the draft or moves it to run/. Existing task associations are reused.
Completion offers task basenames; duplicate basenames require a full listed name.
