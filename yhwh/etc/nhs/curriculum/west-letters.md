# The West · Paleo-Hebrew alphabet

| Order | Glyph | Answer | Aliases | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 𐤀 | aleph | alef; alif; ʾ | The first letter. It represents a glottal stop. |
| 2 | 𐤁 | bet | beth; beit; b | Think b as in boat. |
| 3 | 𐤂 | gimel | gimmel; g | Think hard g as in go. |
| 4 | 𐤃 | dalet | daleth; d | Think d as in door. |
| 5 | 𐤄 | he | heh; hey; h | Think h as in house. |
