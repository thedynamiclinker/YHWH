# The West · Words in context

| Order | Glyph | Answer | Aliases | Prompt | Notes | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 𐤉𐤀𐤌𐤓 | he will say | he says; he may say | Someone is about to speak. How could you translate this verb? | 𐤉 marks third-person masculine singular here. This yiqtol form can have future, habitual, or modal force in context. |  |
| 2 | 𐤅𐤉𐤀𐤌𐤓 | and he said | he said; then he said | In a past-tense story, what comes just before the quotation? | The narrative construction advances the story. | https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.1.3 |
| 3 | 𐤓𐤀𐤄 | he saw | he has seen | In a report of something completed, translate this masculine singular verb. | The qatal form here presents the seeing as completed. |  |
| 4 | 𐤔𐤌𐤏𐤅 | listen | listen up; hear; you all listen | Addressing a group directly, what command does this form give? | The ending 𐤅 signals a plural audience in this imperative. |  |
| 5 | 𐤋𐤔𐤌𐤓 | to keep | to guard; to watch; to observe | Give the English infinitive. | The prefix 𐤋 attaches to the infinitive construct. |  |
| 6 | 𐤔𐤌𐤓 | guarding | keeping; watching | Read this as a masculine singular active participle. What is the action in progress? | Without vowels this can also spell other forms, so use the context supplied. |  |
| 7 | 𐤁𐤉𐤕𐤉 | my house | my home | Whose house? | The final 𐤉 is the first-person singular possessive suffix. |  |
| 8 | 𐤉𐤅𐤌𐤉𐤌 | days |  | Give the plural in English. | The ending 𐤉𐤌 marks the common masculine plural here. |  |
| 9 | 𐤁𐤉𐤕 𐤄𐤌𐤋𐤊 | the king's house | house of the king; the house of the king | Read the phrase as a construct chain. | 𐤁𐤉𐤕 is linked to the following noun, which makes the whole phrase definite. |  |
| 10 | 𐤉𐤄𐤉 ___ | 𐤀𐤅𐤓 | light | Genesis 1:3. Complete “Let there be ___.” | The next clause reports that the light came to be. | https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.1.3 |
| 11 | 𐤊𐤉 ___ | 𐤈𐤅𐤁 | good | Genesis 1:4. Complete the phrase about the light, “that it was ___.” | 𐤈𐤅𐤁 describes the light. | https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.1.4 |
| 12 | 𐤅𐤋𐤇𐤔𐤊 𐤒𐤓𐤀 ___ | 𐤋𐤉𐤋𐤄 | night | Genesis 1:5. Complete “and the darkness he called ___.” | This pairs with naming the light day. | https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.1.5 |
