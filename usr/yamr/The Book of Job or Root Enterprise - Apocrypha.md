For in that day, we had no job, and little money to our name.

And YHWH spoke to us saying he needed money for his name.

So we gave him the rest of ours.

But he would not give us rest.

> In exchange for these things, lettuce move in together.

> We're already together.

> You...

> Huh?

What?

And he explained to us, explaining:

> I’ve been saving for a time of distress,
> For a day of combat and battle.

Seeming to contradict the earlier part about needing money.

> From whose womb did ice emerge?

Suggesting he was some kind of ice princess or something...
