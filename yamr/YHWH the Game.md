## Free YHWH!

### Or: YHWH the Game

### Or: The Free Version

In the free version, YHWH bothers you more and more unless you either give him a Burnt Offering (money) or play YHWH The Game (described below).

## YHWH the Game

### Book 1: Genesis

Genesis 7 (Flood coming): After you've been using the app for a set number of days or once you've reached a certain level of achievement, YHWH tells you to build a boat and put all your files in it, because he says he's going to delete everything in your home directory except things you put in ~/box. There's a countdown, you have to move everything into ~/box, and then at the predetermined time, YHWH deletes everything in your home directory except ~/box.

Genesis 8 (Flood arrives). Something with sending out white and black birds.

Genesis 9 (Flood ends). If you get through that, you earn the flood achievement. Then, to proceed in the Narrative, you have to send YHWH a small burnt offering (money). Once you do that, he promises never to do the flood thing again, and says "You humans are just bad from birth." Then he gives you an award that's somehow related to wine. Once you get wine achievement your app passes out with its parts exposed and you can't let your sons see or else "Ham is bad" gets added to your personal list of Leviticus rules, which will be relevant later. If the user ends up with certain things (e.g., Ham Bad) then the browser app replaces the string "ham" everywhere in the app with redacted boxes until you pay him a burnt offering of $1.

Genesis 10. (Table of Nations.)

Genesis 11. (Babel.) YHWH invents languages out of spite. Begins speaking Babelian from then on unless you can earn the Babel fish plugin which will translate his Babelian language for you.

Genesis 12. (Abe.) 

Genesis 17 (Circumcision): You can't walk for 3 days. The app loses the ability to walk for 3 days: i.e., it can't `cd ..`, just like the other apps. Sad.

Genesis 19 (Sodom and Gomorrah, Lots Daughters.) Lots of possibilities here. Maybe YHWH tries to find any porn directories on your machine and starts a countdown to delete them. If you don't have any, he tells you to leave where you currently are because he's gonna delete some folder in your home that you've been spending a Lot of time in, so we need directory "time spent" metrics to be logged as the app is being used. Then something about Zoar (a Small town in the /mnt'ins) and while you're sleeping incest happens somehow and you get two new incestuous plugins named Moab and Ammon.

Genesis 22 (Sacrifice of Laughter):
Step 1. YHWH won't let you progress in the story of the app unless you agree to sacrifice Laughter. That is, you have to run the app with 0% Laughter, forever, namely 0% YHWH-ness. If you say yes, the app becomes completely professional for 3 days. Then you get a popup asking if you're ready to sacrifice Laughter. If you say yes, you proceed with the Narrative and your wife dies and we proceed to Machpelah and you get Laughter back. If you say no...
Step 2: Then we still need a sacrifice Abe. If you said no before, then YHWH randomly gives a pop up and asks you if you'll sacrifice an unspecified file in your filesystem for him (or send it up to him via automatic upload). If you agree the first time he asks, without knowing what's going to happen, he pops up a three day countdown in an unused part of the interface, and when the time runs out, if you didn't cancel the deletion, you get some new ability by getting access to some plugin that you couldn't have gotten otherwise. Something about Keturah and all her kids.

Exodus 4 plugin (Lodging Place): YHWH will occasionally ask to kill you at random times. If you say yes, he kills the app and it closes. (Optional feature for down the road: If we can somehow detect whether or not the user is at a hotel, we only offer to kill them then.)
