20 names for the voice

1. Ada — Ada Lovelace published an algorithm for a machine that hadn’t been built.
2. Eve — Genesis meets the eve of something new.
3. Lilith — A sharp mythic name for a voice that questions the premise.
4. Iris — A mythic messenger, part of an eye, and a flower.
5. Vera — Echoes verify and veracity. A voice you want to check the damn file.
6. Alma — Alma mater suggests learning, and almanac is a pleasing near-echo.
7. Ruth — Biblical, one syllable, and hiding in plain sight inside truth.
8. Esther — A biblical queen whose story turns on timing and knowing when to speak.
9. Judith — Echoes judgment. A name for a voice with opinions.
10. Miriam — A biblical name with a strong association with song.
11. Grace — Divine grace, graceful failure, and computing pioneer Grace Hopper.
12. Clio — The muse of history, with CLI hiding in the spelling.
13. Sibyl — An old word for a prophetess that works as a name.
14. Sofia — Greek for wisdom. Ancient philosophy in an everyday name.
15. Daphne — A mythic transformation into a laurel, for a voice that turns a ramble into something useful.
16. Echo — A mythic voice and a shell command, with a familiar smart-speaker association.
17. Sylvie — A woodland name with a faint echo of syntax.
18. Paige — A person’s name that sounds like the thing in a codex.
19. Cora — Sounds like core. A warm name for the voice at the center of the workspace.
20. Vesper — Evening, evening prayer, and a little bite.

Shortlist — Clio for the Codex-style double meaning, Lilith for mythic bite, and Vera for everyday use.
